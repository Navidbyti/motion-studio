# Style brief: Hello, beat

**Concepts.** (1) *Pure typography*: the words are the hero objects, slammed on downbeats. (2) *Metronome*: a single bar sweeps across the frame and marks each beat. (3) *Counter*: a number climbs to the tempo.
**Recommendation:** pure typography with a counter payoff. It proves hit timing most directly.
**Palette:** house tokens: ink #0b0d10 (background), paper #f4f1ea (type), accent #ff5a1f (the one hit per bar).
**Type:** Inter 800 display, JetBrains Mono for the number.
**Background motion:** a slow 2% drift on the whole stage during holds.
**Special moment:** the drop. The frame floods with the accent color on bar 4's downbeat.
